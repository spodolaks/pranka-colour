Gaze of Mountain

Home / Series / Gaze of Mountain

And if you gaze long into an abyss, the abyss also gazes into you ©

Mountains can share mesmerizing views with the one who observes, mountains can display the immense force of ever-changing weather. Mountains can be the most peaceful place one moment and summon all of its demon guards the next. One mountain can remind you of your first love, long forgotten, another can be intimidating like a huge predator. There is something supernatural about mountains. And one day we uncovered that some pictures of the peaks have far more to display, hidden from bypasser, mystical essence, deity that has been present forever.
