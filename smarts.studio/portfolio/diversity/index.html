Diversity

Home / Series / Diversity

Photo series that are going to help us expose our feelings about diversity of life. Looking around it may seem that everything and everyone are grey and dull, and even worse - similar. As a matter of fact things significantly brighter, there is an extreme variety of sides in everyone of us. With “Diversity” we uncover just two sides of each participant, however with hundred participants planned we intend to show a bigger picture.

Nowadays, when we stuck between millions of opportunities and with us humbly staring at them, often unable to make a step, it is important to understand what it is really, that drives other people, what is meaningful to them, what are they crazy about, how it leads them through life. Diversity is not about opposites, it's about variety of passions.

We are calling out everyone to look inside themselves, and try to understand what is kicking your motor. And be brave about it, present it to others!

Be open, be bold.
Be Diverse.
