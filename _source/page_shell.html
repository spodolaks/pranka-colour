How a job runs

STEP 01

Send three frames

Not the whole shoot - three files that represent it. I grade one of them free and send it back. If the look is wrong, it is wrong after one frame instead of after a hundred.

STEP 02

Agree the look, then the set

Once the reference frame is signed off, everything else is matched to it - not by running one preset over the take, but frame by frame until nothing drifts.

STEP 03

Changes, then delivery

Marked up per frame or across the set: warmer, less contrast in the shadows, crop this one wider. Two rounds included, delivered in the format and colour space you publish in.

The part worth paying for is the hundredth frame

Anyone can make one picture look good. The work is making a hundred look like they came from the same afternoon, and doing it on a deadline.
